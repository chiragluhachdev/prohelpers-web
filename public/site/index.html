FAQ

Questions,answered.

As soon as you confirm a request, verified helpers nearby are alerted straight away and have 60 seconds to accept. Most bookings are matched within a minute, and you can watch it happen live in the app.

Yes. Every helper completes Aadhaar-based identity verification and submits supporting documents, which our team reviews before their account is approved. Only approved helpers can receive work.

Pay your helper in cash once the work is done. You'll see the full breakdown — service charge, platform fee, GST and any surcharge — before you confirm, so there are no surprises.

You can cancel free of charge any time before the job starts, straight from the booking screen. Your helper is notified immediately.

When your helper finishes, a code appears in your app. The job only closes once you share that code with them — so nothing is marked complete without your say-so.

Download the app and choose "I'm a Helper" when you sign up. You'll add your details, verify your identity, upload documents and pick the services and area you want to work in. Once our team approves you, requests start coming through.
